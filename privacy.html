Privacy Policy for Vietnam Jigsaw

Effective date: October 21, 2026

Vietnam Jigsaw respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use the Vietnam Jigsaw mobile application.

1. Application Information

Application name: Vietnam Jigsaw
Package name: vn.thecuong.vietnamjigsaw

Vietnam Jigsaw is a jigsaw puzzle game featuring landmarks, destinations and cultural scenery from Vietnam.

The application does not require users to create an account or sign in to use its main game features.

2. Game Data

Vietnam Jigsaw may store game-related information locally on your device, including:

  • Puzzle progress
  • Completed puzzles
  • Collection progress
  • Game settings and preferences
  • Purchase status for features such as removing advertisements

Local game data may be removed by clearing the application's data or uninstalling the application.

If you choose to connect Google Play Games, Google may process your Play Games player profile, player identifier, leaderboard scores and related game activity. The main puzzle features remain available without creating a Vietnam Jigsaw account.

3. Advertising

Vietnam Jigsaw may display advertisements using Google advertising services, including Google Mobile Ads.

Google and its advertising services may process technical information such as device information, IP address, app interactions, diagnostic information and advertising identifiers where permitted.

Advertising behavior may vary depending on device settings, consent, app configuration and applicable legal requirements.

4. Analytics

Vietnam Jigsaw uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is used and to improve gameplay, reliability and performance.

The application may send analytics events such as:

  • A puzzle being started or resumed
  • A puzzle being completed, including piece count and elapsed time
  • A successful Google Play Games sign-in
  • A successful leaderboard score submission

Analytics data may include app interactions, puzzle identifiers, scores, country or region code, device or app identifiers, device and app information, and diagnostic or technical information. Vietnam Jigsaw does not include your name, email address, message content, photos, audio, precise location or payment card information in these custom analytics events.

Analytics information is transmitted using encrypted network connections and is processed by Google under its applicable terms and privacy practices.

5. In-App Purchases

Vietnam Jigsaw may offer optional purchases, including an option to remove advertisements.

Payments are processed through Google Play. Vietnam Jigsaw does not directly collect or store payment card information.

Google Play may provide purchase information necessary to verify, restore or manage purchases.

6. Internet Access

The application may connect to the Internet to access game content, advertising services, Google Play services and other resources required for application functionality.

7. Third-Party Services

Vietnam Jigsaw may use third-party services including:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google Play Games Services
  • Google Play Billing
  • Google Mobile Ads
  • Google Analytics for Firebase

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable legal requirements. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy and Firebase Privacy and Security.

8. Children's Privacy

Vietnam Jigsaw is intended for users aged 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so the matter can be reviewed.

9. Data Security

Reasonable technical measures are used to protect information handled by the application and to use secure network connections where supported.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Game data stored locally may generally be deleted by clearing application data or uninstalling Vietnam Jigsaw.

Information independently processed by third-party services is subject to the retention and deletion policies of those service providers.

You can manage or delete Play Games profile data through your Google Account or Google Play Games settings. To ask a privacy question or request deletion of data controlled directly by the developer, contact the email address below.

This Privacy Policy may be updated when application functionality, third-party services or legal requirements change.

Updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
